Known limitations

Spotlight

1. Only one spotlight can be on per helicopter. Turning one on from another seat replaces
   the one that was on.

2. The manual and tracking beams are sent to players within 700 m of the helicopter or of the
   lit spot (STREAM_RANGE in server.lua). The forward searchlight is the helicopter's own
   built-in light, so it is only visible while the helicopter itself is streamed to you.

3. Other players see the manual spotlight about a tenth of a second behind the operator's
   camera. That is the update rate plus normal network delay and cannot be removed entirely.

4. The radius keys only change the manual spotlight. The tracking beam has a fixed width;
   brightness applies to both.

5. The manual spotlight works from any seat, but only while looking through the camera. The
   forward searchlight and the tracking spotlight (including pause) are switched by the pilot.

6. A spotlight is switched off when whoever turned it on leaves the helicopter or the server.
   This is checked once a second, so it can stay on for up to a second after they leave.

Camera and lock-on

7. Only vehicles can be locked on to and tracked, not people on foot.

8. There is no permission check. Anyone sitting in a listed helicopter can use the camera,
   lock on to vehicles and operate the manual spotlight.

9. Only the models listed in Config.HeliModels (config.lua) are recognized. Add-on
   helicopters have to be added there.

10. The postal code on the display needs the nearest-postal resource. Without it the rest of
    the display still works and the postal shows "---".

General

11. OneSync is required. The server reads vehicle and player positions and stores the
    spotlight in a state bag on the helicopter.

12. Changing a default key in config.lua only affects players who join for the first time.
    Everyone else keeps the key they already have until they change it under
    Settings > Key Bindings > FiveM.

13. The streamed spotlight has not yet been tested in-game with several players at once.
